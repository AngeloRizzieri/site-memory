💡 How to highlight PDFs

  1. Click "Open PDF Directly" above
  2. Select any text in the PDF
  3. The highlight popup will appear automatically
  4. Press Ctrl+Shift+S to see all your highlights
Note: For local PDFs, make sure you've enabled "Allow access to file URLs" in extension settings.
