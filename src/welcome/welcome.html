How to highlight
1

Select text on any page

Use your cursor to select any text like normal.

2

Right-click → Highlight, or press Ctrl Shift S

A small color picker appears. Pick a color to confirm.

3

Click any highlight to edit or add a note

Change color, add a note, or remove it.

4

Open sidebar to browse all highlights

Press Ctrl Shift S with no text selected, or click the extension icon.
