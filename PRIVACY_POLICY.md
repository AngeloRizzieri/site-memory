# Privacy Policy

This extension collects and stores user-selected website content (such as highlighted text) solely for the purpose of providing the extension’s core functionality: saving and restoring highlights on websites chosen by the user.

## Data Collected
- Highlighted website content
- Website URL associated with the highlight

## How Data Is Used
All collected data is used only to enable the extension’s features.  
No data is sold, transferred, or shared with third parties.

## Data Storage
Data is stored using Chrome’s extension storage and remains under the user’s control.  
Users may delete their data at any time by removing highlights or uninstalling the extension.

## Data Sharing
The extension does not sell, transfer, or use data for any purpose unrelated to its functionality.
